Privacy policy

For the apps of Games of Dharma, Paths of Dharma and Anatomy of Illusion. Last updated 30 September 2026.

In short: the apps work offline, have no accounts and no servers of their own, and never sell or trade your data. What you write and play stays on your phone. The only data that leaves it goes to Google, to show a few ads that are never personalized.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (the day's questions, your answers, streaks), your own notes and readings, your highlights, bookmarks and reading position, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Backups

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android keeps a copy of the app's data in your own Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. You can also make a backup file yourself ("Backup & restore") and save it wherever you choose. We never receive either.

What you choose to share

"Share on WhatsApp" and "Export notes" send only the text you see, only when you tap them, to the app you choose. We never see it.

Ads, and what Google receives

The free versions show a few ads through Google AdMob: at most one full-screen ad a day when the app starts (some games add one more when the day's first game begins), and, while you read a text, a small banner below it that never covers the text. To show an ad, Google's software on your phone sends Google technical information such as your IP address, the device model and Android version, and which ads were shown or tapped. Google uses it to deliver ads, count them and prevent fraud.

The apps limit this as far as AdMob allows:

  • Ads are never personalized: no advertising profile of you is built or used (Google's restricted data processing is always on).
  • The apps do not use your advertising ID, and have no access to Android's ad tracking features (Topics, Attribution).
  • Ad content is limited to a family rating (PG).
  • No other ad networks, data brokers or analytics services are used. Data goes to Google only.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and can change your choice under "Privacy choices".

"Remove ads"

The one-time purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that it was bought, which is kept on your phone. Payment details stay with Google.

Children

The apps are made for ages 13 and up. They collect no personal information themselves from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Questions: write to the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
